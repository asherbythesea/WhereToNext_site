User Guide

Documentation
1. Installation

Run the .exe file as Administrator. The installer will automatically detect your MSFS Community folder path.

2. First Flight

1. Launch MSFS 2024 and sit on the tarmac.

2. Open WhereToNext.

3. Click [Connect Sim] in the top right.

4. Your aircraft and location will sync automatically.
