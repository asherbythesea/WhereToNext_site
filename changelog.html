Changelog

Version History

v1.0.5 Current Jan 31, 2026
  • Runway width now displayed in job tooltip (e.g., "Asphalt - 5,000 ft × 100 ft").
  • Improved flight debrief dialog with cleaner layout and route display (KDEP → KARR).
  • Navdata validation on startup - checks file integrity and verifies key airports exist.
  • Improved navdata setup dialog with detailed step-by-step instructions for Little Navmap.
  • Flight debrief "Close" button now navigates to Dashboard with proper sidebar sync.
  • Bush planes now get more destination variety (increased randomization, runway cap 3500ft).
  • Fixed marketplace showing "Insufficient funds" when player had enough money until page was refreshed.
  • Fixed pilots getting runways too small for their aircraft type; added runway width filtering (< 60ft filtered for standard aircraft, bush planes exempt).
v1.0.4 Jan 31, 2026
  • Fixed issue where free flight mode incorrectly triggered when taking off with an accepted job (but without clicking "Begin Flight").
  • Fixed landing detection incorrectly marking job flights as "Landed at destination" regardless of actual landing location. Job flights now only complete when landing at the correct destination airport. Free flights continue to track landings at any airport.
  • Fixed free flight mode not checking if the aircraft is owned by the user. Free flight now only tracks when flying an aircraft from your hangar.
  • Fixed marketplace "Buy" button not responding to clicks.
  • Fixed issue where buying multiple aircraft at once would not update the infobar/notification area.
v1.0.3 Jan 29, 2026
  • Improved the onboarding experience to recommend more atypical starting locations.
  • Fixed the scaling issues with the new onboarding dialogs.
v1.0.2 Jan 29, 2026
  • Added a feature where the current job will begin if you forget to hit "Begin Flight" if you would have passed the Pre-Flight Briefing anyways.
  • Reworked landing and takeoff detection; accounting for errors within a 15 second grace period.
  • Overhauled pilot location persistence, allowing for free flight mode to gain XP but not money. Starting a flight from your location, without taking a job will trigger a free flight.
  • Added current aircraft and current location to the Dashboard page.
  • Improved how waypoints can be detected in the route plan and placed on the map in the Current Job page.
  • Fixed issue with Departure and Arrival airports not being labeled on the job board.
  • Fixed issue with the Logbook and Marketplace pages not loading on first attempt.
  • Fixed issue where the UI would lag during an update.
v1.0.1 Jan 26, 2026
  • Added additional aircraft.
  • Improved job generation algorithm.
  • Current Job page revised.
  • Converted all aircraft to use metric in the database.
v1.0.0 Jan 21, 2026
  • Initial non-public release.
