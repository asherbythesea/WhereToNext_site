WhereToNext

Overview
The Project

WhereToNext is a career companion application built specifically for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.

Explore the world by earning money and XP through completing jobs, leveling up your pilot, and purchasing new aircraft.

Why Another Career Application?

The default Microsoft Flight Simulator career mode often fails to provide a consistent experience for many pilots. Many pilots who choose to use external Air Traffic Control software or use the VATSIM network, clash with the built-in MSFS 2024 career mode.

Existing third-party career applications have their issues as well. Features are locked behind a paywall, the application framework is bloated or outdated, or is overly complicated for someone who just wants to pick an available job and fly there.

Key Features

Performance

WhereToNext is built with WinUI3.

  • Proper HiDPI scaling
  • Low memory usage
  • Fast, smooth experience

Job Generation

  • Intelligent job creation based on your aircaft and location.
  • Explore the world by always having a new place to fly to.

Progression

  • Increase your pilot level to unlock more aircraft.
  • Use your logbook to track your progress and landing performance

No Paywall

  • Free to use, forever.
  • Keep your wallet safe from another subscription burning your wallet.
  • No ads either!
