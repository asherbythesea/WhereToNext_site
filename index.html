WhereToNext

Overview

The Project

WhereToNext is a pilot career application built specifically for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.

Explore the world by earning money and XP through completing jobs, increasing your pilot level, and purchasing new aircraft.

Why Another Career Application?

The default Microsoft Flight Simulator career mode often fails to provide a consistent experience for many pilots. Many pilots who choose to use external Air Traffic Control software or use the VATSIM network, clash with the built-in MSFS 2024 career mode.

Existing third-party career applications have their issues as well. Many are hidden behind paywalls, built on outdated frameworks, or burdened with unnecessary complexity. This application was built to solve these problems: a modern, lightweight, and streamlined career experience for pilots who just want to pick a job and fly.

Key Features

Performance

WhereToNext is built with WinUI3. Proper HiDPI scaling, low memory usage, fast smooth experience.

Job Generation

Intelligent job creation based on your aircraft and location. Explore the world by always having a new place to fly to.

Progression

Increase your pilot level to unlock more aircraft. Use your logbook to track your progress and landing performance.

No Paywall

Free to use, forever. Keep your wallet safe from another subscription. No ads either!
